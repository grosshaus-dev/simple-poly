# Simple Poly

This is a collection of simple poly models for games, the collection is still in its infancy, which means the models are not considered stable. Individual models may still change drastically and the naming and structure of the project is not complete.

*Simple poly* is used to refer to 3D artwork that contains the a simplified amount of polygons. Depending on the artists vision it may not necessarily be low poly.

*Simple poly* is not *low poly*, in fact *simple poly* models can often have quite a high poly count, as all the details are modeled and not added by textures.

Rules for *simple poly* models:

- The less important or interactive an object is the larger the single flat faces should be and the less overall detail the model should contain.
- The more important or interactive and object is the smaller the flat faces should be and the more detail it should contain.
- Solid colors are to be used instead of textures; textures are not allowed.
- Normal maps may be used to add the needed detail to things like water.
- Edges may be smoothed or hard, depending on the object and the artistic vision.

- Perfect circles are to be made with 8 sides.

## Known Issues

- There are errors with smoothing that have not been addressed. Some of the models need to be re-exported to fix this.

## Supported Engines

The models are intended for use with Unreal Engine and are named to comply with the [Recommended Asset Naming Conventions](https://docs.unrealengine.com/5.2/en-US/recommended-asset-naming-conventions-in-unreal-engine-projects/) for Unreal Engine.

That said, they *should* work with any game engine.
